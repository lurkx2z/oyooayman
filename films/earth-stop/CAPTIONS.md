[title] What if Earth stopped spinning for 1 second?

[start] 5:42 PM, on the equator. The ground just stopped turning.

[first] East of town, a hill stopped with it. / Nothing else did.

[speed] This station always moves east at 1,670 km/h. You never feel it, because everything moves together. / And something just got in front of the Sun.

[bolts] The station was bolted to the ground. It didn't matter. / To stop with the ground, its steel would have to be 50 times stronger than steel is. / So every bolt sheared. The building is sliding off its own floor.

[town] It isn't only the station. / Every building in town just left its foundations. / So did every parked car, every lamp post and the top of every palm tree.

[people] Nobody on the platform has had time to react. They're doing 1,670 km/h, standing still. / The platform can only slow them by 25 km/h each second. And it ends in 20 metres. / They leave its end still standing up. They'll touch the ground 220 metres later. / The pigeons are fine. They were already in the air, and the air came too.

[train] Your train weighs 180 tonnes. Its wheels can't spin up this fast, so they skid. / Steel skidding on steel is a weak brake. Inside, it feels like the driver touched the brakes. / The record for a train on wheels is 575 km/h. Yours is doing 1,670.

[energy] At this speed, every kilogram carries the energy of 26 grams of TNT. / Your train carries almost 5 tonnes of it. It's harmless, until something stops it.

[hill] 600 metres east of the station, the line runs into the hill. / The hill is part of the ground. It stopped. / The depot's shed just got there.

[depot] Six wagons of fuel were parked 210 metres from it. 80 tonnes each. / That's 2 tonnes of TNT per wagon, before the fuel. / The first one just reached the rock.

[wind] The air didn't stop either. Against the hill it's a wind at 1.3 times the speed of sound. / Where the rock stops it, the air heats by 108 °C. / It's pinning the fire to the hill and driving it over the top.

[sea] Behind the station is the harbour. The sea didn't stop either. / This is what got in front of the Sun. / Every metre of this quay is being hit by 4,800 tonnes of water. / The ferry was 290 metres out. It weighs 2,000 tonnes.

[up] The water can only go up. It's climbing at over 1,000 km/h. / This is happening on every west-facing shore on Earth.

[fall] One second is only long enough to fall 4.9 metres. / The station's roof was 5.6 metres up. It's still in the air. / So is the woman who was on the footbridge.

[sky] Planes fly in the air, and the air never stopped. / Right now, the safest place on Earth is 10 kilometres above it.

[count] Your train has slid 410 metres. The hill is dead ahead. / Nobody on board has heard a thing. The hill is coming faster than its own sound. / The ground starts turning again in 0.05 seconds.

[back] 5:42:01 PM. The ground is turning again. / Everything still in motion is suddenly standing still. / Your train is 150 metres short of the hill.

[twist] But the ground stopped the fire, the air and the sea. They have no speed left. / And the ground is doing 1,670 km/h again. From your seat, all of it was just thrown west. / It reaches your train in 0.2 seconds.
