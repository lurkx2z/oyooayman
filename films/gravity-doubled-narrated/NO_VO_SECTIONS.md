# The sections without narration

The brief asks for deliberate silences: the narrator introduces an idea, then stops so the audience can watch the
physics happen. These are the speech-free sections, measured from the real clips (last spoken word → next first word).
No subtitles are on screen in any of them; the music comes up and the sound effects carry the action.
