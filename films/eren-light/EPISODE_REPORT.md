# Episode report: Would Eren and Light agree?

Narrated 2D story film, 16:9 1920×1080, 30 fps, **5:49** (349.1 s), 94 shots, 48 voice clips (narrator Michael,
Eren, Light). Branch `claude/project-thread-dlj9ks`, slug `eren-light`. Plan and structure: `PLAN.md`.

## What the owner asked for
- "now make would eren and light agree?" (same narrated format as *Know everything*).
- "I've seen a lot of stickman channels that look exactly like ours. Make the graphics extremely unique, our own
  distinct style, extremely good, levels above the others."

## What changed in the look (versus Strong bones / Know everything)
- **No stickmen and no flat vector look.** Every frame is painted, then printed through an ink shader: screentone
  dots, ink outlines, warm paper, and only two colours allowed at full strength, Light's red and Eren's green. The
  argument is colour-coded from the first frame to the last (the four eyes in the dark).
- **Real close-ups.** A new bust renderer draws both leads at portrait size (cel shading, eyes that act, brows,
  mouths, glowing slit eyes, sweat, wet hair). 26 of the 94 shots (about a quarter of the running time) are
  portrait close-ups, two-shots or eye shots, like a manga page, instead of tiny figures in wide sets.
- **Manga grammar.** Speed-line and tone backdrops, panel splits, torn round cards, AGREE / CLASH stamps on a
  scoreboard, impact frames (the page flips to negative on the biggest hits), sound-effect lettering.

## Retention build
- Action in the first second (a pen writing a name, a man falls 40 s later, a wall of giants), title at 0:30.
- A clear frame: four rounds with a scoreboard, so the viewer always knows where the argument is.
- Two rules set up on screen at 1:30 and paid off at the end (the 40-second countdown versus Eren's future).
- The last minute: a red counter that runs exactly 40 real seconds from the last pen stroke, 17 s with no narration,
  rain and heartbeat, the smile, lights out, one question for the comments. No recap.

## Sound
Original score and effects, synthesised (no library music): cold-open drone, the Light motif (piano and choir), the
Eren motif (brass), a march for the giants, organ for "god", the round hits, a tubular bell at the clock's first
second, a 60 bpm piano pulse into the countdown, near silence for the last 17 s (ticks, heartbeat, rain at
−25.6 LUFS), the zero boom and a D-major choir under the smile. Master −15.3 LUFS, ≤ −1.5 dBTP. In the gaps between
lines the music sits about 20 dB over the rain (the house rule: music leads, ambience under it).

## Checks
- Every frame (10,473) drawn once without errors; start and end frames of all 94 shots checked on contact sheets.
- The runnable ZIP was unzipped into a clean folder and played in Chromium: one click starts the picture and the
  sound together.
- Timing: the countdown's 40 s is exact (name finished 4:52.26, "Forty" 5:32.26); clock ticks land on its seconds.

## Weak spots (honest)
- No independent review round was run on this film; I checked it myself on stills and contact sheets.
- I cannot listen: the mix is checked by measurement only (loudness per section, music-to-rain balance, peaks).
- The full-body figures in the wide station shots still use the simpler rig (stiffer limbs and hands than the
  close-ups). The giant is a suit-shaped silhouette, not a detailed titan. Light's hair reads a little dome-shaped
  in some angles.
- Under 30 MiB the halftone dots soften slightly; the full-quality master is the better upload if size allows.
- The leads are recognisable by design cues only (by choice, no copied art), so a viewer who does not know the two
  series relies on the narration to know who they are.
