# Episode report — *What if you were in the North Tower on September 11, 2001?*

**Format.** 16:9 long-form, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 6:08 (368 s). Voiceless, in the house format of *Slower light* and
*Universe expanding faster*: 82 short second-person serif captions, a corner readout, a clock, and the sound. The
register is different: a memorial, not a spectacle. Page `north-tower.html`, code `films/north-tower/`, captions
`CAPTIONS.md`, every fact in `RESEARCH.md` (9/11 Commission Report, NIST NCSTAR 1 and 1-7, FEMA 403, the 9/11 Memorial
& Museum), plan and review log in `PLAN.md`.

**The idea.** You are one of the people who worked below the impact zone of the North Tower: a composite of
survivors' accounts, on the 73rd floor, 20 floors under the impact. The film follows your morning by the real clock,
from 8:41 to 10:29, and the real way out. The point of view is the evacuation that worked: almost everyone below the
impact in the North Tower got out (about 7,500 people), down the stairs, helping each other, past the firefighters
going up.

## What the film does not show, on purpose

No plane. No one falling, no one hurt on screen, no bodies, no gore. No collapse closer than a survivor on the street
saw it (the tag on screen says so). No voices with words. No politics, no perpetrators, no names.

## Platform note (YouTube)

- YouTube allows 9/11 content that is educational, documentary or memorial and avoids graphic imagery; this film is
  built to that standard. It may still get **limited ads** (the "tragic events" ad-suitability guideline), so treat
  revenue on it as uncertain.
- Title: keep it a question about the place, not survival ("What if you were in the North Tower on September 11,
  2001?"). Avoid "survive", "escape", "challenge" or "POV game" in the title and tags.
- Thumbnail: the glowing stairway (glow paint in the dark) or the 8:41 window over the clear sky. Not a burning tower,
  not the collapse, no faces.
- Description: lead with the memorial; say the person you follow is a composite of survivors' accounts; list the
  sources (9/11 Commission Report, NIST NCSTAR 1, the 9/11 Memorial & Museum); add a short content note ("depicts the
  September 11 attacks; no graphic imagery").
- Audience: set "Not made for kids". Hold comments for review (9/11 videos attract conspiracy comments).
- It is animation, so YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" label is not needed.
- Length 6:08: under 8 minutes, so no mid-roll ad breaks (fine for this one).

## Still weak

- Visuals are the lowest score (5 after round 2): the people are simple low-poly figures, the night skyline at the end
  is a block city, and the office windows read as grey blocks from some angles.
- The stair shots from 1:33 to 2:35 share one high angle down the flight; there is no close-up of the water bottle.
- The office ceiling shows gaps in its tiles before 8:46 (they should only appear after the jolt).
- The label-and-circle markers on the towers ("THE NORTH TOWER · YOUR TOWER") look a little like a game.
- The corner readouts are still small on a phone (the captions are fine).
- The round-2 fixes have not been through a third review.
