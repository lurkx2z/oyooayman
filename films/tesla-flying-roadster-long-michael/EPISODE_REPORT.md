# Episode report — What if your car could fly? (long cut, 16:9)

**Film:** `tesla-flying-roadster-long-michael.html`, 486.9 s (8:07), 1920×1080, 30 fps. Charlie narration, 53 clips.
**Ask:** "make it long form 8 min video" (2026-10-17). A separate film; the 2:32 vertical short is unchanged.

## What's in it
Cold open on the demo → the claims → slow-motion replay → three ways to beat gravity:
1. **A wing**: inverted flight is possible, hanging still is not (lift ∝ v², the stall). Race cars and the "drive on a
   ceiling" idea, and the catch: slow down and the grip goes. A toy loop, the loop speed, then a flat ceiling where
   nothing pins you up.
2. **Suction**: 2 g ≈ 7 kPa, under a tenth of air pressure; the fan-car history (Chaparral 2J 1970, Brabham BT46B 1978,
   McMurtry Spéirling 2025); suction needs a surface.
3. **Thrust**: Newton's third law with an astronaut; cold gas, 600 L, ten nozzles; the roll and the braking burst.
Then what if it tried to hover (≈ 18 kN, ≈ 30 kg/s, seconds of air), the helicopter and the bottle rocket, the hop over
the cars; a reality check (shown vs promised, 2017 → 2028 timeline, the FAA); and a closing "you": a passenger hanging in
the harness, then the upside-down POV.
