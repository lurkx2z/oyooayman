## PHYSICS RULE
At 1.2 s the surface tension of liquid water (72 mN/m at 20 °C) starts to fall; it is 0 by 1.6 s and stays there.
Everything else is held constant: water's density, viscosity and chemistry, how strongly it sticks to solids, every
other liquid, gravity and air. On screen: "WATER ONLY · EVERYTHING ELSE NORMAL" and the end note "FICTIONAL RULE: ONLY
WATER'S SURFACE TENSION CHANGED / TIME COMPRESSED". Consequences shown, one per beat: no drops (beads and a domed glass
slump into films, a tap stream frays instead of pinching off), nothing floats on the surface (paperclip, water strider),
no capillary action (paper towel, sponge, soil, the pull that lifts water up a plant), rain can't stay in drops, and
water-repellent fabric lets water through (its entry pressure scales with surface tension). Full rules and what the film
must not claim: `PLAN.md` § 1.

## NEW SYSTEMS
All film-local and `nst`/`Nst`-prefixed, in `films/no-surface-tension/`:
- `water.js`: Fresnel water material, `NstPuddle` (a drop spreading into a film by Huppert's gravity–viscous law),
  `NstStream` (a ballistic tap stream that twists and frays), `NstSpray` (soft mist), `nstWetLook`, `nstSpreadR`.
- `kitchen.js`: `NstKitchen`, the kitchen set with its own reflection map: glass with a domed top and beads, overflow
  film, gooseneck tap and steel sink, glass bowl and paperclip (with a faint "would float here" outline), paper towel,
  sponge and its drain.
- `garden.js`: `NstGarden`, sky by day, night and storm; pond with veined pads and striders; ripple rings; potting bench
  with the watering run-off; flower bed, reeds, trees and lawn that wilt over three days; torn-strand rain; park and city.
- `film.js`: hand poses (some aimed at world points), the watering can and its stream, the umbrella and its soak shader,
  world tags ("NORMAL WATER WOULD …"), the drone rise with lightning, the colour grade.
- `audio.js`: water's voice is drops (drips that stop, then only a hiss), a taut string that goes slack, glass tink,
  garden birds that thin out, a warm pad that sours, rain with no patter, thunder, a closing chord.
- `script.js`: every beat, the cut, the words and the camera.

## KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- The hand's skin tone shifts between the kitchen and garden lighting.
- The dome and spill on the dark counter are subtle; they read best on a bright phone screen.
- The towel beat shows the absence of wicking but no "would be wet to here" band.
- The umbrella shot shows only the canopy (no shaft or hand); the soak is a shader effect, not simulated water.
- The time-lapse readout still overlaps the top-left of the tree; the bench shot's lower third is empty.
- Rain is stylised (torn ribbons and haze), not a fluid simulation.
- The sound was level-measured only (−17.0 LUFS integrated, true peak −2.1 dBFS, LRA 6.8 LU); no one has listened to it.
- Bubbles, soap, tears and ink are deliberately left out for the comments (brief).

## PERFORMANCE / FPS
- Per frame: 160–1160 draw calls (including shadow passes) and 63–91 k triangles; the garden storm shots are the
  heaviest (rain strips, wilting plants, park and city).
- The final render ran headless on a software renderer with 4 workers: 1810 frames in about 69 minutes (the kitchen
  chunks were the slowest per frame). Real-time playback in Chrome on a GPU was not measured here; the kitchen shots are light,
  and the garden is in the same range as the other city films.
- Everything is a pure function of time (seeded, no `Math.random`, no wall clock), so any frame renders identically.
