# Episode report — *What if sound kept getting slower and couldn't stop?*

**Format.** 16:9 long-form, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 8:05 (484.5 s). Voiceless, in the Slower light / Universe shrinking
format (the owner's brief, 2026-10-26: that formula, accurate physics, about 8 minutes with no filler, fresh, not a
remake of the old 9:16 "Slow sound" short, which kept one constant slow speed). The film is told by 130 short
second-person captions, a corner readout (THE SPEED OF SOUND · what moves that fast · NEXT landmark and its time), a
clock, illustrative rings and the sound itself. Page `slower-sound.html`, code `films/slower-sound/`, every number in
`PLAN.md` (computed in `physics.js`), captions `CAPTIONS.md`.

**The idea.** From 7:00 on a Saturday the speed of sound halves every hour and never stops. One ordinary day at home and
on the high street, where every sound you hear is rendered physically: its real delay through the slowing air, the
pitch drop of sound that slowed on the way, Doppler, and the reversed order and crack of anything faster than sound.
Sound that is already flying slows too, so every sound gets a **horizon**, the farthest it can ever reach: 56 km at
noon, 870 m at 6 PM, 14 m at midnight, 21 cm by dawn. Then: is any of this real, and a question for the comments.

## Sound

Synthesised and seeded, three stems. Every diegetic sound is placed physically: `ssHeardStill` (a still source: delay
through the slowing air and the frequency drop c(arrival)/c(emission)) and `ssMoving` (a moving source: for each
moment you hear, which emission moments are reaching you; Doppler, reversed order and the crack of a supersonic source
fall out of that). So the bell really arrives 75 s late, the music box 294.8 s late at ×0.945, the TV 4.48 s late, the
siren ×2.6 then ×0.62. The score's hour chime drops an octave every hour until it is below hearing; the music box's tune
is the film's theme.

**Loudness (measured, ffmpeg ebur128):** mix −15.8 LUFS integrated, LRA 10.3 LU, true peak −1.4 dBTP (the evening and
night lifted 4–6 dB after a first measurement at −15.5 LUFS with LRA 13.0 and stretches near −30 LUFS).
`final-soundtrack.m4a` (AAC 256k): −15.9 LUFS, −1.0 dBTP. `small-soundtrack.m4a` (limited, AAC 128k): −16.2 LUFS,
−1.6 dBTP. The page plays the baked copy (`soundtrack.js`) at once.
