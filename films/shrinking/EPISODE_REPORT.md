# What if you kept shrinking and couldn't stop? — episode report

Voiceless 16:9 long-form film, 1920×1080, 30 fps, **7:38** (458 s). Page `shrinking.html`, code `films/shrinking/`,
plan and physics in [`PLAN.md`](PLAN.md). Made after the owner's study of a channel that posts 8-minute "What if" films
with no narration (one continuous first-person journey, short captions, a corner readout, a human thread underneath).
Every caption, scene and sound here is our own.

## Fixes made after the reviews

1. **Wrong or confusing readout comparisons** (science): the corner said things like "as tall as an atom" at a few
   femtometres. Now it only says "as tall as" within a quarter of a landmark, otherwise "smaller than"; the bedroom
   comparison uses real sizes (city, country, Earth, Sun, Neptune's orbit, Milky Way, visible universe); the edge of
   measurement is named "the closest any experiment has looked".
2. **Captions that overclaimed or confused** (science + viewer): the atom section was rewritten (no surface, a blur
   of maybe, the stadium and the pea shown with a real stadium outline and a marked pea), the proton's weight claim
   corrected ("only 1% … is the quarks"), the speculative ending labelled SPECULATION, the molecular hail labelled
   SLOWED DOWN A BILLION TIMES, the Planck-scale lines softened to "if theory is right" / "may".
3. **The opening was slow and the hands covered the room** (viewer): the cat beat moved earlier (it watches you from
   0:17), the first 30 s shrink faster, the hands are lower and smaller, and the HUD label no longer hides the NEXT line.

Also: the spilt water reads as a glassy wall now (it was nearly invisible), the bacteria twitch and get shoved like
you do, the furniture undersides in the look-up catch the floor's bounce light, the quake shakes the camera, and
caption backing is darker for small screens.

## Sound

No narrator. A music-box theme (A–F–E–D) that returns an octave lower in each world, then a falling Shepard tone below
the proton that freezes at the Planck length; ambience that grows with you (the clock gets deeper, the cat's breath
becomes wind); effects placed from the same motion as the camera (footsteps, mite feet, shoves, hail). Mix:
measured on the full render at **−15.0 LUFS integrated, loudness range 7.5 LU, true peak −1.3 dBTP** (the page's
baked soundtrack); the masters for the final MP4 are limited after AAC encoding to −15.2 LUFS / −2.4 dBTP (256k) and
−15.3 LUFS / −2.4 dBTP (128k, the small copy).
