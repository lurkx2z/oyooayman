[title] What if magnets got 1,000 times stronger for 10 seconds?

[start] Every magnet on Earth just got 1,000 times stronger. / 10:42 AM. For the next 10 seconds.

[radio] Your radio just went silent. It's still on. / Its speaker's magnet is now so strong it holds the paper cone almost still.

[phone] Your phone is full of tiny magnets. / The scrapyard's magnet is pulling it out of your hand. You hold on.

[pile] That magnet usually lifts one car at a time. / Now it's lifting the whole pile. / The pile pulls back just as hard. The crane's rope just snapped.

[reach] A magnet's field fades with distance cubed: 10 × 10 × 10 = 1,000. / So the same field now reaches 10 times as far, and pulls steel from 8 times as far. / Your boat is 20 metres from it.

[mri] Across the river, the hospital's MRI scanner is usually 3 tesla. / Now it's 3,000. A fridge magnet is 0.005. / Its field now pulls steel from about 6 metres away, straight through walls.

[cylinder] An oxygen cylinder was standing in the corridor. 15 kg of steel. / It's coming through the wall.

[water] Magnets push water away, very weakly. In 1997, a 16-tesla magnet floated a live frog. / At 3,000 tesla, the water in that cup is thrown out of it. / A patient in there would be pushed out at up to 5,500 g.

[waiting] The waiting room is 10 metres away. Every phone in it is pulled at about 2.4 times its weight. / And each one is wrenched round like a compass needle, out of people's hands.

[speed] Back at the scrapyard, steel is hitting the magnet at up to 780 km/h. / To get away again, a bolt would need 530 km/h. Nothing does.

[boat] Your boat is 12 tonnes of steel, 20 metres from that ball. / It's sliding sideways. Only the water is holding it back. / Halve the distance, and the pull grows about 40 times.

[real] Officially, this is impossible. / A magnet's two ends now squeeze each other with 4 million atmospheres, more than at Earth's centre. / A real magnet would tear itself apart. These hold.

[cars] Those old cars weigh a tonne each, 15 to 25 metres away. / The nearest are flying. The rest are rolling.

[road] Most electric cars have a ring of magnets in their motor. / Now it fights the wheels like a brake. / On the bridge, every electric one is skidding.

[turbine] Those wind turbines make power with giant magnets. / They stopped dead. Their 10-tonne blades didn't. / They're still falling.

[boat2] Your mum is steering away with everything the engine has. / The ball is already pulling 8 times harder than the engine can push. / Still 17 metres from it. The engine is losing.

[inside] Inside the boat, the tins and pans are starting to slide toward the yard. / You aren't. The iron in your blood isn't the kind a magnet can grab. / The kettle just slid off the stove.

[phonehit] Your phone is now pulled at 5 times its weight. You can't hold it. / It just hit the ball at 1,800 km/h.

[close] You're less than 9 metres from the wall. / At 2 metres, the ball will pull up on the boat harder than it weighs.

[count] In 3 seconds, every magnet goes back to normal. / There goes your bike. / It's pulling the boat out of the water.

[back] 10:42:10 AM. Back to normal. / Your boat drops back into the river. / The ball lets go. Its outside falls away. / Its middle stays one lump, crushed harder than any car crusher could.

[after] Everything it pulled keeps a little magnetism now. / Including your boat. Paperclips will stick to its hull.

[question] How many magnets are you holding right now?
