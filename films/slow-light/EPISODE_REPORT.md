# Episode report — *What if light kept getting slower and couldn't stop?*

**Format.** 16:9 long-form, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 5:57. Voiceless (the owner chose captions and sound only on
2026-10-18): the film is told by 90 short second-person captions, a corner readout and the sound. Page
`slow-light.html`, code `films/slow-light/`, plan and every number `PLAN.md`, captions `CAPTIONS.md`.

**Where it comes from.** The owner picked the premise and asked for a hybrid of four successful "What if" channels
(study: `/mnt/project-files/what-if-channels-study/STUDY.md`). What the film takes from each: one quantity that keeps
falling with a corner readout and a plain comparison ("AS FAST AS A PASSENGER JET"); a new familiar landmark every
20–40 s; short setup-then-punch captions with empty screen between; a human thread underneath (Gran, the cat, Mum's
text); one evening, first person, in a flat on a harbour; real physics, then the real experiment at the end. Every
caption line is our own.

## Reviews (independent, first cut) and what changed

**Viewer review:** enjoyment 6/10, retention 5/10, clarity 6/10, visuals 6/10. Strongest: the fireworks ladder, the
dark spilling from the lamp and the egg link, the blue corridor, the house cut-away. **Science review:** science 7/10,
clarity 7.5/10; it recomputed the delays, Doppler directions, the egg's foci, car 2's first sight (b/√3 up the road,
0.9 s after it passes) and the 1999/2001 figures, and found them right.

The fixes below were made after those reviews and have **not been re-scored**.

Science fixes:
1. *The last light* said it was "a millimetre from your eye", but light 1 mm in front of the cornea still has the whole
   eye to cross. Now: "The next light you'd see… is already inside your eye. It never gets there.", with a large diagram
   of stopped light inside the eye, a hair from the retina.
2. *The lab* overclaimed. Now "Slow light is. In a lab."; "Inside them, light slowed to 17 metres a second."; "2001:
   they stopped a pulse of light. Stored it in the atoms."; "As fast as a car." (61 km/h); the probe beam goes dark while
   the pulse is stored.
3. *The knock*: the readout said 0.41 km/h under "Slower than a tortoise" (0.27 km/h); c is now 0.065 m/s there, and
   "your hand … late" is computed, then counts down.
4. *Mum's text*: "still travelling" next to "NOT MOVING". Now "stuck 675 metres away" (computed).
5. *Car 1*: "its brake lights go deep red" (×0.8 puts red LEDs in the infrared, and it isn't braking). Now "the whole
   car turns red".
6. *Fireworks*: each gap was set from c at the moment of the flash, breaking the film's own rule that light already on
   its way slows too. c is now held in steps solved so that ∫ c dt over every 2 km trip is exact; the stopwatch runs on
   the real pair times.
7. *The premise*: the label now says "THOUGHT EXPERIMENT · ONLY LIGHT SLOWS · WIRES AND CHIPS STILL WORK".
8. *Car 2*: "One is the car" → "One shows it driving on" (both images are its past); new caption "Like a jet outrunning
   its own sound."; the diagram from above. Smaller: "7½ times round the Earth", an ILLUSTRATION label on the map, the
   egg's bounce points on the floor line, the cat's sprint 0.9 s (about 25 km/h, still under the dot first).

Viewer fixes:
1. *The dark act*: the motes are gone; the eye diagram fills the frame; the x-ray of the flat now starts at the front
   door, so Mum is seen coming in, and the bulbs light up at "They're not."; the act is 3 s shorter.
2. *Car 2*: the readout says what you hear and see ("HEARD: 51 m AWAY · SEEN: NOTHING"); your eyes follow the sound
   past you; the split is a wide still shot; then the diagram; the images are labelled on screen.
3. *Fireworks*: tighter lens (bursts about three times the area), a flash → bang stopwatch with SEEN / HEARD chips,
   a jolt on every bang, and the white column on the water (the flash light's glint) is gone, so the captions read.
4. *The cat*: the camera closes in as she runs (field of view 44° → 24°), a bigger beam head and dot.
5. *The call*: pushes in as Gran falls behind and again for her laugh (now a bigger laugh).
6. *The knock*: a warm key light on the hand, a closer frame, a countdown to seeing it.
7. *The readout*: the NEXT rows are gone; a soft backing plate; small lines at 22–26 px (1080p); the honest label
   moved to the bottom left; Mum's text pops when it's sent.
8. *The title*: now three lines, ending "AND COULDN'T STOP?"; the first frame is no longer black.

**What feels recycled (own review).** The flat is the main set for most of the film (living room, balcony, hall); the
street is the only exterior. The readout plate is the same in every scene by design. The x-ray cut-away is the one
overhead shot. The score's theme comes back often (on purpose: it slows as light does).

## Sound

Score, room sound and effects are all synthesised (`sound-*.js`); the voices are speech shapes with no words you can
make out. Every sound happens when it really happens (sound is never slowed), never when it is seen: that gap is the
story. Mix measured on the final soundtrack: SEE BELOW.

## Still weak

- Car 2 is the hardest idea in the film; the diagram helps, but the live split itself lasts about a second before the
  two images leave the frame (that is the physics at this speed and distance).
- The egg and the lab's "stored in the atoms" are ideas a younger viewer may still lose.
- Hands, the cat and Mum move stiffly; Mum is small in the overhead shot.
- The flat repeats as a set; the ending card is static.
- The mix was checked by measurement and spot listening of stems, not by watching the whole film with sound.
