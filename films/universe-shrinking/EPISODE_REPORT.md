# Episode report — *What if the universe kept shrinking and couldn't stop?*

**Format.** 16:9 long-form, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 4:44 (284.2 s). Voiceless, in the Slower light / Universe faster format
(the owner's brief on 2026-10-25: the exact formula of those films, a fresh escalation, and retention first: "people's
watch time are almost half of the video length"). The film is told by 76 short second-person captions, a corner readout
and the sound. One quantity keeps getting worse and can't stop: **the temperature of space**. Page
`universe-shrinking.html`, code `films/universe-shrinking/`, every number in `PLAN.md` (from `physics.js`), captions
`CAPTIONS.md`.

**The idea.** Tonight at 7:30 the universe starts to shrink, faster and faster, and it can't stop: at midnight there is
no space left (a Big Crunch). You are in your back garden on a freezing night with a telescope and your cat. On the way
to midnight the universe rewinds its whole history, ending as the light of the Big Bang. Then: is any of this real
(DESI 2025, one study's Big Crunch in about 20 billion years) and the garden at 7:29 again.

## Reviews (independent, first cut) and what changed

**Viewer review:** enjoyment **6/10**, retention **5/10**, clarity **6/10**, visuals **5/10**. Strongest: the fire run,
the bird bath callback, the last second. Drop-offs it named: 0:20–0:49 (abstract), 0:53–1:08 (near black), the
galaxies-crashing side-track, a washed-out ending, a 32 s epilogue; broken: white rectangles in two cosmos shots, a
"−0 °C" reading, DAWN against twilight, a double bath label, a white disc and streak on the lawn, panels washing out,
five text blocks at once.

**Science review:** science accuracy **8/10**, clarity of the science **7.5/10**. It re-ran `physics.js` and agreed
with every caption's clock time (s = 2 at 9:13:39, 0 °C at 11:27:51, 100 °C at 11:32:39, brighter than noon 11:41:37,
the Sun's surface 11:54:58, the Planck temperature 1.7×10⁻¹⁹ s before midnight) and the 2025 claims (DESI DR2 at
2.8–4.2σ; Luu, Qiu & Tye: a crunch about 20 billion years from now). Errors it ranked: the last-second readout ran past
the Planck temperature; "if it weakens enough, the growth turns around" (a fading push still grows forever: it must
become a pull); a next-milestone label "ICE MELTS · AT 11:27" (that is space passing 0 °C); "the sky would end just like
this" (in a real crunch nearby starlight is barely squeezed); a wrong reason on the galaxies' light; nuclei burst at
about 5 billion K, not 1 billion; protons pinned at 1 trillion K against a ladder of 2 trillion; "physics runs out"
(known physics).

of the universe in the last second), the garden at night (town, owl, dogs, a dawn chorus at twilight), the hot wind,
