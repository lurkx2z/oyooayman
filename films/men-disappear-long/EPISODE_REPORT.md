# Episode report — *What if all men suddenly disappeared?*

**Format.** 16:9 long-form, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 6:09 (368.6 s). Voiceless, in the Gravity keeps tilting / Friction 10s /
Air solid / Speed of light formula, built to the owner's YouTube lessons of 28 October 2026 (an everyday place, action
in the first seconds, a real-world nod in the first minute, about six minutes, tension to the last frame), the rule of
29 October 2026 (**the story clock only runs forward**) and "more new scenery": no kitchen, flat, street or city set
reused. One clock (`MG_CLOCK`, `script.js`) runs from Tuesday 10 November 2026, 7:41:57 AM, to 10:21:46 AM: real speed
under the title, the first 15 story seconds after 7:42 slowed up to 13 times (the HUD says how much), real speed from
the radio on, with forward jumps only at cuts (+7 min, +13 min, +66 min, +21 min, +16 min, +27 min, +6 min, each on
the HUD). `script.js` throws if the clock runs backwards, if a caption's clock time or fuel figure disagrees with the
HUD, or if the clock jumps during the cockpit door's 30 seconds; `shots.js` throws if it jumps anywhere but on a cut.
The film is told by 67 short second-person captions, the HUD (the clock, the men-and-boys counter, flight 2207's fuel
under the clock from 3:01 on, one context box per beat, insets), labels pinned in the scene, honest REAL/ROUGH source
notes and the sound. Page `men-disappear-long.html`, code `films/men-disappear-long/`, every number in `PLAN.md` § 2
(computed in `physics.js`), captions `CAPTIONS.md`.

**The one impossible thing.** At 7:42:00 every man and boy alive vanishes, with what he is wearing; whatever he held
stays and falls. Boys not yet born stay. Everything else follows from ordinary physics and from how the world is
staffed: who drives, who flies, who controls the traffic, who fights fires, who delivers babies, who leads. The film
says so on screen ("THE ONE IMPOSSIBLE THING …").

## How it follows the owner's lessons and playbook

- Everyday place, human scale: the passenger seat of your dad's car on the morning commute. Everything else is seen
  from the viaduct or cut to as "meanwhile". All new sets: a motorway viaduct across a river valley, a car's interior,
  a lorry cab, a train cab, a barge, an airliner (outside, cockpit, cabin), a control tower, a delivery room.
- Action on frame 1: your dad is driving under the title; at 0:04 his seat is empty and the cup is falling.
- Real-world nuance in the first minute: holding an electronic parking-brake switch while moving is an emergency stop
  (car manuals), then the honest "one impossible thing" label.
- A stake by 0:40: the lorry drifts into the barrier right in front of you; "THE LORRY: 3.0 m AHEAD".
- No clock resets: one forward clock; the slow seconds say how slow, every jump says how far.
- About six minutes (6:09), no recap; flight 2207's fuel runs under the clock from 3:01 to the landing, and the last
  shot opens the bigger question over 23 planes still circling.
- Respect: nobody is hurt on screen and no body is shown; the sign is an empty seat; fires are smoke seen from afar; the
  warmest scene is a birth. The facts are about jobs and numbers, not blame; the women in the film are the ones who act.
- Teaser Shorts: the vanishing and the lorry (0:04–0:55), the cockpit door's 30 seconds (2:17–3:01), the birth
  (3:36–4:05), the landing over the motorway (5:24–6:01).

## Reviews

One round, three independent reviewers on the full cut (contact sheets and stills of every shot, the caption
timeline, the facts), before the fixes. Scores as given:

- **Story and retention 7/10**: a real hook, fact and stake inside 40 s and a strong fuel thread, but the HUD counter
  ("MEN AND BOYS ON EARTH 2") contradicted "every man and boy just vanished" and spoiled the ward's twist; four
  low-stakes "a machine keeps going" vignettes after the lorry (radio, phone, train, barge); flight 2207 off screen
  for 96 s in the middle; the attendant passive in the autoland captions; number captions too fast to read.
- **Picture 6/10**: the wide exteriors work (the viaduct aerials, the smoke, the airliner, the ward, the 9:10 walkers);
  z-fighting on every cabin seat; the planes in the sky unreadable at phone size (an empty blue frame, a beaded
  contrail, 10 px circling planes); the barge and river unreadable; his phone's screen floating off its body; the
  vanishing shot unreadable; the cockpit-door sequence crude and cropped; HUD boxes over subjects; captions over other
  text; a pale "pillar" (the motorway cutting) on the horizon; clouds like lens smudges.
- **Accuracy 7/10, respect and tone 9/10**: four facts wrong or out of date (the population split was backwards, the
  "three autopilots" of a twin like flight 2207, the leaders count, "they're all still here" for midwives); some
  numbers unsourced or overstated; one line on the police needed rewording.

Fixed after the round: the counter now says MEN AND BOYS VANISHED until the birth, then counts the boys born since
7:42; 4.17 billion men and boys (just over half; UN WPP 2024), "both autopilots", 28 of 193 countries (UN Women, 1
January 2026), "nearly all are still here", England's firefighters, ICAO's controllers, ROUGH labels on every
estimate, "in many newer cars", "as far as we know" on the autoland claim; captions split and re-cut to reading pace,
the attendant made active ("She finds each switch"), the fuel line under the clock from 3:01 to the landing, and "MOST
HAVE ABOUT 1½ h OF FUEL" on the last shot. Picture: the seat covers wrap the seat backs (no z-fighting), flight 2207
centred at the head of a continuous contrail, the circling planes grey, unfogged and larger with strobes and beacons,
cameras re-aimed for the stack and the last shot, the river tinted and the barge on a long lens, his phone's screen on
its body and the phone on the floor by the spilt cup, the vanishing re-framed on his empty seat, the cockpit-door
cameras re-placed (the keypad, her wait, the open door), HUD boxes cleared (planeMap, fuel, radar tags placed without
overlaps), the flaps HUD waiting for the wheels, the last approach framed over the motorway, a tower camera with a
clear line to the plane, the cutting faded into the hills, fuller flat-bottomed clouds. These fixes were checked on
stills, not re-scored.

## What is still weak

- Story 7/10, picture 6/10, accuracy 7/10 and respect 9/10 were the scores before the fixes; the fixes have not been
  re-scored.
- The barge beat was kept (the reviewer suggested cutting it); 1:12–1:56 is still four "machines keep going" moments,
  now at real speed.
- Flight 2207 is not seen between 3:22 and 4:37; only the fuel line under the clock keeps it present.
- The train cab, the lorry cab and the people are the house's simple low-poly blocks; the circling planes in the
  wide sky shots are small (they read as planes at 1080p, as dots on a phone).
- The radar is busy: with 24 planes some tags sit far from their blips.
