# How did kids have fun before screens? — production plan

90 s · 9:16 (1080 × 1920) · 30 fps · first-person · same engine and look as *What if oxygen suddenly disappeared?*

**The idea in one line:** with fewer ready-made forms of entertainment, many kids turned ordinary places, objects and other people into play. It's not "old times good, phones bad".

**Setting:** a stylised composite small town, around 1905, temperate climate:
- painted clapboard houses with porches;
- a few brick shopfronts;
- a dirt street with stone gutters;
- yards, picket fences, elms, gas street lamps;
- a church steeple in the haze.

It doesn't represent one real city. Narration says *many kids*, *games like these*, *on streets and in yards*, never *all children*.

**The viewer:** a modern kid of about 10, with an eye height of about 1.3 m. They keep their own hoodie sleeves in the past: you are the one who was transported. That makes the ending (the same hands, back home) land.

## 2. Timeline structure

- `films/before-screens/script.js` holds the whole film as data:
  - story events (named times);
  - keyframed tracks (exposure, light, time of day, imagination);
  - the camera path (x, z, eye height, yaw, pitch, fov);
  - hand poses;
  - the HUD script (title, stacked lines, captions, info blocks, end line);
  - character performances (paths and actions per child).
- **Scenes are sets in one world**, so cuts are just camera jumps (deterministic and scrub-safe). The bedroom set, the town, the meadow and the parlour are built far apart in the same three.js scene. A cut moves the camera; a `step` key makes it instant.
- **Lighting per set:** a `daylight` track (sun angle, colour, sky, fog) runs from afternoon → sunset → night. Sets that aren't on screen are hidden (cheap).
- Everything is a pure function of `t` (seeded RNG), as before.

## 5. Reused from the What-If engine (no rewrite)

- **Timeline and story:** `Timeline`, `Track`, `SmoothTrack`, easings, seeded `RNG` / `hash` / `noise`.
- **Camera:** `CameraController` with child eye height, walking/running bob, breathing, sway, startles and shakes. It now reads optional tracks, so it works without the oxygen ones.
- **Hands:** `ViewerHands`, the authored arm, now takes options (scale, colours, watch, extra poses).
- **Post-processing:** `PostProcessing` (AO, bloom, ACES, warm-keep, vignette, grain, fades). The film now supplies its own colour grade, and the flash colour is configurable (warm white for the door light).
- **Fog:** `installFog()` (uneven exponential haze), with warmer, softer settings for this film.
- **Materials:** `Look.surface` selective gloss (marbles, glass, metal).
- **Characters:** the people rig (lofted parts, pose blending, walk / jog / kneel / sit actions) and `BlobShadows` contact shadows.
- **Effects:** `BillboardSystem` (dust puffs, fireplace sparks).
- **Geometry and textures:** `Tex` canvas textures and `Geo` / `Batcher` static merging.
- **Audio:** `AudioEngine` (offline mix, sync, WAV export, spatial listener) and `SoundKit` synth blocks.
- **HUD and tools:**
  - `StoryHUD`, new and shared: title, stacked lines, captions, info block, end line;
  - fonts, dev controls, recording mode;
  - `tools/render-preview.cjs --page before-screens.html`.

## 7. Historical sanity check

| Fine for c. 1905 | Avoided |
|---|---|
| Marbles (clay and hand-made glass swirls; machine-made glass appears around then) | Motor cars (rare and noisy, they'd steal focus) |
| Spinning tops with string, hoop and stick, jacks (jackstones) | Telephone and power poles |
| Jump rope, hopscotch (chalk), tag, hide-and-seek, stickball | Electric street lights, signage in modern fonts |
| Paper-and-stick kites | Plastic anything, sneakers |
| Safety bicycles, handcarts, horse-drawn wagons (distant sound only) | Bright saturated "toy" colours |
| Gas street lamps with a lamplighter; kerosene / oil lamps and fireplaces indoors | Sepia filters (the past is in colour: green grass, blue sky, painted wood) |
| Checkers, playing cards, picture books | |

**Clothing:**
- boys in knickerbockers with stockings, caps, braces, sailor collars on the younger ones;
- girls in dresses with pinafores and hair ribbons;
- boots, with some kids barefoot in summer.

We imply, rather than lecture, that childhood also involved chores: a girl carrying a bucket, washing on a line.
