==================================================
CORE CONCEPT
==================================================

TITLE:

WHAT HAPPENS
IF YOU SLIP
AND FALL?

This is a SELF-PARODY of our What Happens If videos.

The audience has repeatedly joked that somehow every scenario eventually ends
in a tsunami.

This video intentionally takes the most harmless possible event:

A PERSON SLIPS ON A WET SIDEWALK

and escalates it through a completely absurd butterfly-effect chain until:

THE WORLD GETS HIT BY A MASSIVE TSUNAMI.

The humor comes from treating this ridiculous causal chain with the EXACT same
seriousness as our normal physics simulations.

==================================================
MOST IMPORTANT COMEDY RULE
==================================================

DO NOT TELL THE VIEWER IT IS A JOKE EARLY.

Do not use:

- funny music
- meme sound effects
- laughing narration
- goofy fonts
- emojis
- exaggerated cartoon animation
- characters winking at camera

Everything should look like a completely sincere scientific disaster simulation.

The audience should gradually realize:

"Wait, there is no fucking way this is where we're going..."

Then:

TSUNAMI.

Only AFTER the tsunami payoff do we acknowledge the joke.

==================================================
SCIENTIFIC HONESTY / SATIRE RULE
==================================================

The causal chain in this video is intentionally fictional and absurd.

Do NOT present it elsewhere as real science.

The visual/narrative style may imitate scientific explanation for comedy,
but the final video should include a small end clarification such as:

SATIRICAL SIMULATION

or:

NO, FALLING OVER CANNOT CAUSE A GLOBAL TSUNAMI.

Keep this AFTER the main joke lands.

Do not ruin the opening with a disclaimer.
