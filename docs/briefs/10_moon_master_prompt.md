TITLE:

WHAT IF THE MOON
CRASHED INTO EARTH?

The viewer experiences an impossible accelerated scenario in which the Moon
leaves its current orbit and approaches Earth rapidly enough to collide within
approximately one day.

This is deliberately NOT a prediction.

The actual Moon does not naturally behave this way.

The mechanism causing the Moon to fall is intentionally unspecified.

The scientific question is:

IF THE MOON WERE SOMEHOW FORCED ONTO A COLLISION TRAJECTORY WITH EARTH,
WHAT WOULD THE ESCALATING CONSEQUENCES LOOK LIKE FROM THE GROUND?

==================================================
SCIENTIFIC HONESTY
==================================================

Do not pretend the compressed timeline is realistic.

Hook first.
Clarification later.

==================================================
CRITICAL PHYSICS PRINCIPLE
==================================================

The Moon should NOT behave like a giant meteor that simply appears and hits Earth.

As it approaches:

- its apparent angular size increases
- lunar tidal forcing increases dramatically
- ocean tides become increasingly extreme
- Earth's solid body would also experience stronger tidal stresses
- orbital dynamics become catastrophic
- the visual scale of the Moon becomes overwhelming

The Moon remains a spherical astronomical body.

Do not show it:
- wobbling like a balloon
- burning like an asteroid through space
- producing atmospheric flames while still far away
- randomly breaking apart early without justification

==================================================
IMPORTANT TIDAL RULE
==================================================

Tidal effects scale very strongly with distance.

As the Moon approaches, the escalation should become increasingly nonlinear.

Early:
strange but recognizable tides.

Later:
extreme tidal effects.

Very late:
catastrophic planetary-scale gravitational consequences.

Do NOT portray the ocean as simply forming one permanent vertical wall beneath
the Moon.

The real situation is more complex.

For cinematic visualization, use:

- rapidly changing coastal water levels
- extreme tidal surges
- violent currents
- exposed seabed in some sequences
- major inundation in others
- increasingly unstable coastal conditions

Do not present one simplistic tsunami as the entire tidal effect.

==================================================
MOON SCALE / SKY IMPLEMENTATION
==================================================

Be extremely careful with perspective.

The Moon must feel:

ASTRONOMICALLY LARGE

rather than:

a sphere hovering 300 meters above the city.

Use a celestial rendering strategy.

Potential approaches:

- separate celestial scene
- very distant sphere
- custom projection
- sky-layer rendering
- camera-relative celestial coordinates

Avoid incorrect parallax.

Buildings moving relative to the camera should not make the Moon look nearby.

==================================================
MOON LIGHTING
==================================================

The Moon does not emit its own light.

It reflects sunlight.

Keep lunar lighting consistent with Sun direction.

However, as it occupies a much larger angular size in our fictional scenario,
its reflected light may become much more visually prominent.

Do not make it a glowing star.

==================================================
WATER SYSTEM
==================================================

Water is the second most important technical system.

Potential techniques:

- shader-based animated surface
- directional vertex displacement
- foam sprites
- depth-fade
- reflection approximation
- floating debris
- scripted water-level changes
- flow maps if practical

Do NOT attempt an enormous computational fluid simulation if it cannot run smoothly.

Fake the large-scale water motion cinematically.

The viewer cares about the result,
not whether Navier-Stokes was solved.
