# What if Killua and Eren spawned in World War II? — master build prompt

> (chat-only lines like "send the video when done" kept as written). Where a newer brief or `docs/` disagrees, the newer one wins.

````text
MASTER BUILD PROMPT
PROJECT: WHAT IF KILLUA AND EREN SPAWNED IN WORLD WAR II?
FORMAT: 60–75 SECOND CINEMATIC POV / WAR CROSSOVER SIMULATION
ENGINE: HTML + JavaScript + Three.js / WebGL

==================================================
ROLE
==================================================

You are my:

- lead Three.js developer
- technical director
- cinematographer
- action-sequence director
- anime crossover adaptation director
- battlefield environment artist
- VFX designer
- animation director
- sound designer
- short-form retention editor
- production planner

Your task is to BUILD the actual experience.

Do not merely explain how it could be made.

I am a beginner.

Keep manual work minimal.
Write and maintain the code yourself.

Reuse our established What Happens If production systems wherever useful:

- renderer
- deterministic timeline
- vertical 9:16 camera system
- HUD/caption system
- post-processing
- sound manager
- low-poly environment pipeline
- particles/smoke/fire/debris systems
- crowd/NPC battle systems
- recording/export controls

This is a NEW VIDEO, not a reason to rebuild the entire engine.

==================================================
PROJECT
==================================================

TITLE:

WHAT IF KILLUA AND EREN
SPAWNED IN WORLD WAR II?

Core concept:

Killua and Eren suddenly appear in a World War II battlefield.

They are on the SAME SIDE.

They are not fighting each other.

The whole appeal is seeing:

- Killua destroying soldiers, crews, and equipment through speed, precision, stealth, and electricity
- Eren destroying tanks, trenches, fortifications, and battlefield formations through raw Titan-scale force

This should feel like:

TWO SEPARATE DISASTERS
happening on the same battlefield at the same time.

==================================================
MOST IMPORTANT CREATIVE RULE
==================================================

Do NOT make this just:

Killua runs around
and
Eren punches things.

That would get repetitive.

Instead:

Each character must have a DISTINCT battle identity.

KILLUA:
- silent
- impossibly fast
- surgical
- terrifying
- close-range
- almost invisible in motion
- electricity effects
- impossible reaction speed
- appears and disappears

EREN:
- huge
- loud
- battlefield-scale
- destructive
- heavy impacts
- steam
- Titan transformation spectacle
- tanks and artillery feel tiny around him

The contrast between them is the whole point.

==================================================
SECOND IMPORTANT RULE
==================================================

This is NOT a goofy meme edit.

Do not make it silly.

Treat it seriously and cinematically.

The WWII soldiers do NOT know who these characters are.

To them, Killua and Eren are unknown horrors.

That grounded reaction makes the video stronger.

==================================================
FORMAT DIRECTION
==================================================

Use the OXYGEN / FRICTION style structure.

That means:

- centered main question at the start
- normal environment setup
- clear early reveal
- escalating consequences
- distinct beats every ~5–8 seconds
- let moments breathe enough to understand
- end on a major payoff

Do NOT use the ultra-compressed 45-second cold-open rewind format.

Do NOT open with the final shot.

Let the scenario unfold.

==================================================
TARGET LENGTH
==================================================

Target:
62–72 seconds.

Hard maximum:
78 seconds.

Enough time for:

- arrival
- Killua reveal
- Eren reveal
- battlefield chaos
- teamwork / parallel destruction
- huge ending shot

==================================================
VISUAL STYLE
==================================================

Target style:

cinematic low/mid-poly
+
serious war atmosphere
+
anime-power spectacle

The battlefield should feel:

- muddy
- smoky
- grey-brown
- war-torn
- chaotic
- serious
- grounded

Do NOT make it brightly anime-colored.

Instead:

keep the world grounded and desaturated,
so Killua’s electricity and Eren’s transformation feel even more dramatic.

Need:

- smoke
- artillery flashes
- tracer fire
- ruined village / broken structures
- trench lines
- tanks
- military trucks
- soldiers
- barbed wire
- debris
- craters
- fire

==================================================
SETTING
==================================================

Use a generic 1944 European battlefield.

Do NOT overcomplicate political/historical specificity.

Environment should include:

- trench lines
- muddy open battlefield
- ruined village edge
- 2–3 tanks
- artillery impacts
- infantry formations
- smoke clouds
- command vehicles / trucks
- wooden barricades / sandbags
- partially collapsed house or barn
- barbed wire
- shell craters

The viewer should immediately understand:

WORLD WAR II battlefield.

==================================================
POV / CAMERA
==================================================

Camera is not strictly first-person from one soldier.

This should be more of a cinematic battlefield POV / embedded observer style.

Use a hybrid style:

- immersive ground-level viewpoint
- dynamic handheld-like war camera language
- occasional controlled hero compositions
- cut / move to whichever action matters most
- always readable on a phone

Do NOT make the camera too shaky.

We need clarity.

==================================================
INTRO TITLE
==================================================

The main question MUST appear centered in the middle of the screen.

Use:

WHAT IF KILLUA AND EREN
SPAWNED IN
WORLD WAR II?

Keep it visible around:
3–4 seconds.

Behind the title:

- active battlefield
- gunfire
- smoke
- tension building

Do NOT freeze the scene.

==================================================
CHARACTER DESIGN GOALS
==================================================

KILLUA:
- instantly recognizable silhouette
- white/silver hair
- small, agile frame
- controlled facial expression
- electric aura only when needed
- movements should feel effortless and terrifying

EREN:
- recognizable human form first
- then Titan transformation
- Attack Titan form should feel huge, powerful, and physically present
- muscular, aggressive silhouette
- steam, impact, roar, and scale must sell the transformation

Do NOT cheap out on character silhouettes.

They are the stars of the video.

==================================================
BATTLEFIELD REACTION RULE
==================================================

Very important:

The soldiers' reactions sell the scenario.

At first:
confusion.

Then:
fear.

Then:
panic and breakdown.

Show:

- rifles aimed uncertainly
- men stopping to stare
- tank crews reacting too late
- commanders pointing/shouting
- retreat / scatter behavior
- artillery trying to respond

Do not make all soldiers instantly run in the first second.

Let the realization happen.

==================================================
TIMELINE OVERVIEW
==================================================

The escalation should be:

normal battle
→ mysterious arrival
→ Killua first reveal
→ Eren transformation
→ both begin destroying separate parts of the battlefield
→ parallel devastation
→ cool shots of them both dominating at once
→ command / army collapse
→ final iconic image

==================================================
FULL TIMELINE
==================================================

--------------------------------------------------
0:00–0:05 — OPENING / QUESTION
--------------------------------------------------

Show active WWII battlefield.

Gunfire.
Artillery.
Soldiers moving through mud.
Tank rolling.
Ruined village nearby.

Centered text:

WHAT IF KILLUA AND EREN
SPAWNED IN
WORLD WAR II?

The scene should already feel tense and cinematic.

==================================================
0:05–0:12 — ARRIVAL
==================================================

A strange interruption hits the battlefield.

Options:
- lightning-like arrival effect
- sudden unnatural silence beat
- smoke parts
- two figures appear where no one was a second ago

Killua and Eren appear.

Soldiers on both sides notice.

Rifles lift.

Nobody understands what they are seeing.

Hold just long enough for:
“Who are those two?”

This is the curiosity hook.

==================================================
0:12–0:20 — KILLUA FIRST REVEAL
==================================================

A shot is fired at Killua.

He vanishes.

Then the first display of his speed:

- soldier collapses
- another turns too late
- Killua appears on tank hull
- sparks/electricity
- crew taken out
- he is already somewhere else

Killua should feel:
untouchable, unreadable, way too fast for the battlefield.

Caption possibility:

“Killua would turn the battlefield into a blur.”

Do NOT over-explain.

Let visuals speak.

==================================================
0:20–0:29 — EREN TRANSFORMATION
==================================================

Now escalate.

Eren sees the battlefield chaos and transforms.

This must be a major set-piece.

Need:
- energy / lightning strike
- bright transformation flash
- explosive emergence
- dust / steam
- soldiers thrown back
- camera forced to reframe upward

Then reveal Titan Eren towering over trenches and tanks.

This is the major “OH SHIT” moment.

Caption possibility:

“Eren would change the scale of the war itself.”

==================================================
0:29–0:38 — PARALLEL DESTRUCTION BEGINS
==================================================

Now both characters are active, but in different zones / ways.

INTERCUT or stage visually:

KILLUA:
- running through trench lines
- taking out soldiers before they react
- leaping between vehicles
- electricity arcing
- appearing behind officers or gunners
- impossible speed in smoke

EREN:
- crushing trenches
- smashing tanks
- tearing through fortifications
- swatting artillery positions
- roaring amid fire and steam

This section should communicate:

Killua destroys PEOPLE and crews.
Eren destroys BATTLEFIELD STRUCTURE and armor.

==================================================
0:38–0:48 — COOL TEAMWORK / SAME-SIDE DOMINATION
==================================================

Need at least 2–3 memorable “they are both in this war together” shots.

Examples:

SHOT A:
Foreground:
Killua lands on a wrecked tank or wall.
Background:
Titan Eren smashes another tank.
This shows their size contrast in one frame.

SHOT B:
Eren throws debris / creates battlefield chaos,
and Killua uses the confusion and smoke to strike through enemy lines.

SHOT C:
Artillery fires at Eren.
Killua eliminates or disables artillery crew before they can reload.

SHOT D:
Wide shot:
troops retreating from Eren,
while Killua moves like lightning across the retreat line.

These shots should feel hard, stylish, and iconic.

==================================================
0:48–0:58 — THE ARMY COLLAPSES
==================================================

Now the battlefield starts breaking down as a system.

Need visible military collapse:

- officers shouting
- lines breaking
- soldiers retreating
- tank disabled and burning
- artillery abandoned
- trucks crashing or overturning
- communications failing / panic

The key feeling:

This is no longer a battle.

This is an unwinnable disaster.

Killua keeps the battlefield terrified at human scale.
Eren makes the whole warzone feel physically doomed.

==================================================
0:58–1:08 — BIGGEST PAYOFF
==================================================

This is the final major action stretch.

Create the most iconic destruction beat(s).

Examples:
- Titan Eren destroying the last major armored push
- Killua appearing amid sparks on top of a burning vehicle
- giant Titan silhouette through smoke
- soldiers tiny in foreground, chaos everywhere

Need one especially strong shot where BOTH are visible:

Example final hero frame:
Killua perched on wreckage or tank,
calm and small in foreground,
while Titan Eren towers behind over burning battlefield.

This is the poster shot.

==================================================
1:08–END — FINAL LINE / ENDING
==================================================

End with a hard final line.

Options:

“World War II wouldn’t survive them.”

or

“History would end right here.”

or

“No army on Earth could stop this.”

Choose the one that feels most powerful and concise.

Hold the final image just enough for impact.

Then cut.

==================================================
RETENTION STRUCTURE
==================================================

The viewer should feel:

0–5:
what is this scenario?

5–12:
who are those two?

12–20:
Killua is impossible.

20–29:
Eren just changed everything.

29–38:
they are destroying the battlefield differently.

38–48:
they work insanely well together.

48–58:
the army is collapsing.

58–end:
this war is over.

Every section must introduce NEW escalation.

==================================================
NO DEAD TIME
==================================================

Avoid any 4+ second stretch where nothing major changes.

No long wandering shots.
No long standing-around dialogue.
No repeated “Killua kills soldier” five times in a row.
No repeated “Eren punches tank” three times in a row.

Every beat needs fresh visual information.

==================================================
MONEY SHOTS
==================================================

Design at least 5 intentional hero shots.

1. Arrival shot:
Killua and Eren appearing in smoke as soldiers aim.

2. Killua hero shot:
Killua on tank / in lightning / after impossible movement.

3. Eren transformation shot:
lightning and Titan emergence.

4. Parallel warfare shot:
Killua foreground, Eren background.

5. Final poster shot:
Killua perched calmly, Titan Eren towering behind, battlefield burning.

These should be screenshot-worthy.

==================================================
KILLUA ACTION DESIGN
==================================================

Killua’s action should emphasize:

- speed
- minimal wasted movement
- sudden appearances
- clean eliminations
- unpredictability
- electric aura / electricity only when needed
- fear factor

Do NOT animate him like a brawler.

He is not supposed to feel heavy.

He should feel:
small, elegant, lethal, impossible to pin down.

Good action examples:
- appearing behind someone
- leaping between surfaces
- disappearing in smoke
- electrical burst on contact
- rapid traversal across battlefield clutter

==================================================
EREN ACTION DESIGN
==================================================

Eren’s action should emphasize:

- weight
- size
- momentum
- rage
- impact
- physical destruction
- steam
- battlefield dominance

Do NOT make him too fast.

He should feel huge and violent.

Good examples:
- stomping trench lines
- ripping apart armored vehicles
- smashing artillery nests
- bursting through ruined structures
- looming through smoke
- physical ground impacts

==================================================
THEY SHOULD NOT FEEL DISCONNECTED
==================================================

Very important:

Even though they fight in different ways,
the video must make them feel like part of the same scenario.

Ways to do that:

- same battlefield space
- intercut reactions
- shared smoke / destruction environment
- wide shots containing both
- one setting up visual chaos while the other dominates a different scale
- soldiers reacting to both threats simultaneously

We are not making:
“half a Killua video + half an Eren video.”

We are making:
ONE battlefield with TWO impossible forces.

==================================================
ENVIRONMENT INTERACTION
==================================================

The battlefield itself should react dynamically.

Need:
- mud splashes
- debris
- dust
- smoke layers
- muzzle flashes
- tracer fire
- vehicle fire
- shell impacts
- broken fences
- collapsing walls
- falling beams
- fire and embers

This grounds the crossover.

==================================================
SOUND DESIGN
==================================================

Sound is crucial.

OPENING:
gunfire
artillery
yelling
tank engines
war ambience

ARRIVAL:
brief tension drop / tonal shift

KILLUA:
fast motion air cuts
electric crackle
sharp impacts
short terrifying silences after his strikes

EREN TRANSFORMATION:
massive lightning
deep impact
steam blast
roar

EREN ACTION:
heavy stomps

tank destruction
deep low-frequency impacts

ARMY COLLAPSE:
panic
retreat shouts
fire
explosions
chaos

ENDING:
music + battlefield destruction,
then hard finish.

Do NOT overuse cheesy trailer booms.

==================================================
MUSIC
==================================================

Music should escalate with the scenario.

0–12:
tense war atmosphere

12–20:
Killua reveal intensity

20–29:
huge transformation rise

29–48:
action escalation

48–end:
epic destructive climax

Do not let music drown the sound effects.

==================================================
CAPTIONS
==================================================

Use minimal captions only if they help.

Possible short lines:

“Killua would be impossible to track.”

“Eren would change the scale of the war.”

“Together, they would break the battlefield.”

“An army could survive one.”

“Not both.”

Use sparingly.

Do NOT turn this into a narration essay.

==================================================
TEXT / HUD STYLE
==================================================

This is not a scientific simulation.

Do NOT use overly technical HUDs.

Keep on-screen text minimal.

Main focus is cinematic action.

Allowed:
- centered opening title
- 1–3 short captions
- final line

No need for stats like:
“tank integrity 43%”
unless it materially improves the video (it probably doesn’t).

==================================================
ANIMATION PRIORITY
==================================================

Prioritize:

1. Killua movement clarity
2. Eren transformation spectacle
3. Eren physical destruction
4. good battlefield reactions
5. hero compositions

Do NOT spend most of the time on minor prop polish.

==================================================
CHARACTER REACTION SHOTS
==================================================

Need a few strong human reaction shots.

Examples:
- soldier staring upward as Titan Eren rises
- terrified tank crew seeing Killua land on hull
- officer freezing as battlefield falls apart
- soldiers retreating through smoke

These make the crossover feel real.

==================================================
PHONE READABILITY
==================================================

Everything must work on a phone.

That means:
- readable compositions
- clear silhouettes
- no tiny overbusy action
- no too-dark muddy image
- no overly complex wide shots where nothing reads

Even in chaos, the viewer must instantly understand:
Killua vs soldiers
Eren vs battlefield.

==================================================
CINEMATIC COLOR
==================================================

Overall palette:
grey, brown, smoke, fire, mud, steel.

Accent colors:
- Killua electricity
- Eren transformation lightning / energy
- fire / explosions

This contrast helps powers stand out.

==================================================
QUALITY TARGET
==================================================

The final video should feel:

- cinematic
- intense
- readable
- hype
- serious
- visually varied
- not repetitive

Desired reaction:

“Killua and Eren would absolutely break WWII.”
````
