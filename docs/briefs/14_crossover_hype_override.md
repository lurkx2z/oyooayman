# Killua × Eren — HYPE / RETENTION / CINEMATIC OVERRIDE

> (chat-only lines like "send the video when done" kept as written). Where a newer brief or `docs/` disagrees, the newer one wins.

````text
==================================================
HYPE / RETENTION / CINEMATIC OVERRIDE
==================================================

Everything in the main master prompt still applies.

However, for this video I want the creative intensity pushed significantly harder.

This should NOT feel like:

"Here is a simulation of Killua and Eren in WWII."

It should feel like:

"Someone somehow made the hardest Killua + Eren WWII crossover edit imaginable,
but inside a coherent cinematic 3D world."

Prioritize:

AURA
ICONIC SHOTS
POWER DIFFERENCE
CAMERA ENERGY
SOUND SYNCHRONIZATION
ESCALATION
EDIT-WORTHY MOMENTS
RETENTION

Do not sacrifice readability for chaos.

==================================================
1. EVERY CHARACTER NEEDS AURA
==================================================

KILLUA and EREN should not merely "perform actions."

Their presence should change the feeling of the scene.

KILLUA AURA:

quiet
calm
almost disrespectfully effortless
tiny compared with the battlefield
but somehow scarier than everyone around him

When Killua appears:

soldiers should feel slow.

Gunfire should feel slow.

The camera should sometimes struggle to track him.

His strongest shots should make viewers think:

"He isn't even trying."

EREN AURA:

massive
violent
heavy
unstoppable
battlefield-scale

When Eren transforms:

the entire visual hierarchy changes.

Tanks that previously looked threatening become tiny.

Soldiers become almost irrelevant.

The environment should react physically to his existence.

==================================================
2. DON'T SHOW EVERYTHING AT NORMAL SPEED
==================================================

Use speed contrast.

For Killua:

normal speed
→ sudden extreme acceleration
→ very brief slow-motion consequence
→ Killua already somewhere else

Example:

bullet fired
→ Killua disappears
→ 0.25 sec slow-motion:
shell casing falling / soldier turning / electric trace
→ normal speed
→ Killua is standing on tank behind them

This makes his speed FEEL faster than simply motion-blurring him constantly.

For Eren:

do the opposite.

Let massive movements breathe.

Punch / stomp:

wind-up
→ huge impact
→ brief low-frequency slow-motion debris moment
→ aftermath

Killua = speed compression.

Eren = impact expansion.

==================================================
3. FIRST KILLUA MOMENT MUST BE HARD
==================================================

Do NOT waste his reveal.

Preferred sequence:

soldier raises rifle at Killua.

Close view:
finger pulls trigger.

MUZZLE FLASH.

Killua is gone.

Bullet crosses where he stood.

Camera whips.

Nothing.

Tank commander turns.

Electrical crack.

Killua is now crouched on top of the tank.

Very calm.

Tiny blue-white electricity around fingers.

Before crew reacts:

BLACK / FAST CUT

tank crew neutralized / vehicle disabled.

Killua looks toward the rest of battlefield.

No celebration.

He immediately disappears again.

This should be a highly replayable moment.

==================================================
4. EREN TRANSFORMATION MUST FEEL LIKE AN EVENT
==================================================

Do NOT make Eren transform with:

small flash
→ Titan appears.

Build anticipation.

Possible sequence:

Eren bites hand / triggers transformation.

0.2 sec silence.

Close-up:
soldier notices something.

Then:

EXTREME LIGHTNING STRIKE.

White-out.

Huge bass hit.

Dust ring expands.

Nearby soldiers thrown backward.

Tank suspension reacts.

Windows in nearby ruined structure break.

Steam fills frame.

Camera struggles upward.

Only then:

Attack Titan silhouette appears through steam.

Then:

EYES / FACE / FULL SCALE REVEAL.

Give the transformation approximately 3–4 seconds total.

It deserves that time.

==================================================
5. TRANSFORMATION REVEAL SHOT
==================================================

One of the most important images:

Foreground:
tiny soldiers / tank.

Midground:
smoke and fire.

Background:
Attack Titan Eren emerging through steam.

Camera low to ground.

Eren's head nearly reaches top of vertical frame.

This shot should make the viewer understand instantly:

THE WAR JUST CHANGED.

==================================================
6. KILLUA VS TANK COLUMN
==================================================

Create one dedicated Killua sequence.

Tank column moving through road / ruined village.

Soldiers believe armor protects them.

Killua appears.

Sequence:

Killua runs ALONGSIDE moving tank.

Touches hull.

Electricity spreads through metal.

Lights flicker.

Crew reacts.

Killua leaps to second tank.

Camera only catches flashes of him.

First tank rolls to stop.

Second turret freezes.

Third tank fires toward Killua.

He's already standing behind it.

Use tank mass against Killua's tiny silhouette.

The contrast should look absurd.

==================================================
7. EREN VS ARMORED PUSH
==================================================

Separate major Eren sequence.

Multiple tanks advance toward him.

Tank fires.

Shell impact creates:

steam
dust
small visible damage

Eren continues.

Second tank fires.

Eren grabs / overturns / smashes one vehicle.

Another tries reversing.

Eren closes distance.

Heavy impact.

Do NOT make tanks explode from every touch.

Use:

crushed metal
overturning
tracks breaking
turrets damaged
vehicles thrown short distances

Weight matters.

==================================================
8. PARALLEL DESTRUCTION MONTAGE
==================================================

This should be one of the strongest sequences.

Intercut rapidly:

KILLUA:
electric flash through trench.

EREN:
tank thrown/crushed.

KILLUA:
appears behind machine-gun position.

EREN:
walks through fortification.

KILLUA:
runs across collapsing wall.

EREN:
roars through smoke.

KILLUA:
lands calmly.

EREN:
huge impact behind him.

Keep cuts approximately 0.8–1.8 sec.

Each shot must be visually distinct.

==================================================
9. CREATE ONE "AURA WALK" SHOT
==================================================

We need one shot that people could repost as an edit.

Killua walking slowly toward camera / across battlefield.

Background:

Eren destroying armored position.

Huge explosion / tank impact occurs behind Killua.

Killua does NOT turn around.

He just keeps walking.

Tiny electric sparks around him.

This is intentionally stylized.

This shot should scream:

AURA.

==================================================
10. CREATE ONE SIZE-CONTRAST SHOT
==================================================

Vertical composition:

BOTTOM:
Killua.

MIDDLE:
wrecked tanks / fleeing soldiers.

TOP:
Titan Eren.

Both visible simultaneously.

Killua looks tiny.

But the viewer already understands he is just as dangerous.

This should probably become the cover/thumbnail candidate.

==================================================
11. TEAMWORK WITHOUT CHEESY TEAM ATTACKS
==================================================

They are on the same side,
but they do NOT need Marvel-style coordinated combo moves.

Their teamwork should emerge naturally.

Example:

Eren destroys bunker / fortification.

Soldiers flee through side trench.

Killua is already waiting there.

OR:

Artillery focuses entirely on Eren.

That creates an opening for Killua to cross battlefield untouched.

OR:

Killua disables anti-armor / artillery crews.

Eren advances through the gap.

They complement each other without needing to shout plans.

==================================================
12. SOLDIERS SHOULD ADAPT
==================================================

Do not keep the armies stupid.

Escalation becomes stronger when soldiers actually TRY different strategies.

First:

rifles.

Fail.

Then:

machine guns.

Fail.

Then:

tank fire.

Fail.

Then:

artillery concentrated on Eren.

Partially affects environment, not enough.

Then:

retreat / regroup.

This shows the battlefield attempting to solve the impossible problem.

==================================================
13. RETENTION BEAT EVERY 5–7 SECONDS
==================================================

Target structure:

0–5:
premise / arrival

5–12:
first mysterious reaction

12–19:
Killua reveal

19–27:
EREN TRANSFORMATION

27–34:
Eren destroys armor

34–41:
Killua destroys fortified position

41–49:
both visible / parallel destruction

49–57:
army escalates response

57–65:
response fails spectacularly

65–72:
iconic final domination shot

There must be no plateau.

Every section should increase:

POWER
SCALE
DANGER
or
VISUAL COOLNESS.

==================================================
14. THE VIDEO NEEDS "EDIT POINTS"
==================================================

Create deliberate moments where:

music beat
+
camera movement
+
character action
+
VFX

all synchronize.

Examples:

BEAT DROP:
Killua disappears.

NEXT BEAT:
electric impact.

NEXT BEAT:
cut to Eren hand bite.

DROP:
transformation lightning.

NEXT:
Titan roar.

NEXT:
tank impact.

These synchronization moments greatly increase perceived production quality.

==================================================
15. DON'T OVERUSE SLOW MOTION
==================================================

Slow motion should only emphasize:

bullet miss
transformation
big Titan impact
one Killua lightning strike
final major destruction

If everything is slow motion,
nothing feels special.

==================================================
16. KILLUA CAMERA LANGUAGE
==================================================

When following Killua:

camera should be:

closer
lower
faster
more reactive

Use:

whip pans
foreground objects
quick reframing
motion blur
electric traces
short cuts

Sometimes deliberately LOSE him.

Viewer sees:

empty frame.

Then:

electric flash somewhere else.

That sells speed.

==================================================
17. EREN CAMERA LANGUAGE
==================================================

When showing Titan Eren:

camera should become:

lower
wider
slower
heavier

Use:

ground-level framing
foreground soldiers
tank silhouettes
smoke
longer shots

Camera shake should correspond to impacts / footsteps.

Do not randomly shake continuously.

==================================================
18. POWER VFX MUST BE CONTROLLED
==================================================

Killua electricity:

blue-white
sharp
localized
fast
thin branching arcs

Do NOT cover entire screen in giant anime lightning constantly.

Eren:

steam
orange-white transformation flash
dust
embers
warm explosion light

Keep their VFX visually distinct.

==================================================
19. BATTLEFIELD COLOR LANGUAGE
==================================================

WORLD:

muddy brown
charcoal
grey
desaturated green
smoke
steel

KILLUA:

cold blue/white electricity.

EREN:

warm white/orange transformation energy
steam
firelight.

This makes both characters immediately readable during chaotic action.

==================================================
20. PARTICLE DEPTH
==================================================

Use particles at multiple depths.

Foreground:

dust
embers
small debris crossing camera.

Midground:

smoke
impact dust.

Background:

artillery plumes
fires
large smoke columns.

This makes the battlefield feel enormous.

==================================================
21. SOUND MUST SELL THEIR DIFFERENCE
==================================================

KILLUA:

brief electrical crack
fast air displacement
sharp impact
silence
another electrical crack

His sequences should sound CLEAN and precise.

EREN:

bass
metal strain
heavy footsteps
explosions
steam
roar
ground impact

His sequences should sound MASSIVE.

==================================================
22. USE SILENCE
==================================================

At least twice.

Before Killua first moves:

brief near-silence.

MUZZLE FLASH.

He's gone.

Before Eren transforms:

audio collapses.

Small breath / trigger action.

Then:

LIGHTNING.

Contrast makes the impact much stronger.

==================================================
23. NPC REACTION DETAIL
==================================================

Don't just animate generic running.

Examples:

tank commander opens hatch and stares at Eren.

soldier slowly lowers rifle when Killua reappears behind unit.

officer tries radio.

soldier trips while retreating.

gunner turns emplacement but cannot track Killua.

These micro-reactions sell the power difference.

==================================================
24. FINAL 12 SECONDS MUST BE THE BEST
==================================================

Do not peak at Eren's transformation and then decline.

Final section should combine EVERYTHING we've established.

Possible sequence:

Enemy launches last armored/artillery push.

Eren advances straight into it.

Killua disappears.

Artillery positions go silent one by one through electrical flashes.

Tanks fire at Eren.

Eren breaks through.

Huge smoke.

Then:

silence.

Reveal:

Killua calmly standing on destroyed tank.

Camera pulls / reframes.

Titan Eren emerges behind him through smoke.

Remaining soldiers retreat in foreground.

Both are visible.

This is the FINAL POSTER FRAME.

==================================================
25. FINAL CAPTION
==================================================

Do NOT use a generic line like:

"They would be unstoppable."

Use something harder and shorter.

Preferred:

"Two people just changed the war."

or:

"The battlefield never stood a chance."

or:

"History ends differently here."

Choose based on tone.

Avoid stating literally that WWII would "end" unless the story actually establishes
that broader consequence.

==================================================
26. DO NOT SHOW GRAPHIC GORE
==================================================

Violence can be intense and destructive.

Use:

impacts
ragdolls
smoke
debris
electricity
vehicles
environmental destruction

Do not rely on gore.

It would make the action less stylish and less reusable across platforms.

==================================================
27. COVER / THUMBNAIL FRAME
==================================================

Create one deliberate 9:16 cover composition:

Killua:
foreground on tank/wreckage.

Eren:
huge background Titan.

WWII battlefield:
smoke, tanks, soldiers.

Clear silhouettes.

Leave some clean visual space for title.

This frame should communicate the entire crossover without explanation.

==================================================
28. REVIEWER INSTRUCTIONS
==================================================

Have independent reviewers evaluate:

AURA:
Do Killua and Eren feel powerful even when not actively attacking?

CHARACTER DIFFERENTIATION:
Does Killua feel fast/precise while Eren feels huge/heavy?

RETENTION:
At what exact timestamp does the video become repetitive?

EDIT QUALITY:
Which 3 shots would people most likely clip/repost/use in an edit?

SCALE:
Does Eren genuinely feel enormous?

SPEED:
Does Killua genuinely feel difficult for the camera to track?

TEAMWORK:
Do they feel like two forces on the same side rather than two disconnected scenes?

FINAL PAYOFF:
Is the last shot stronger than everything before it?

==================================================
29. QUALITY GATE
==================================================

Do NOT approve the final video unless we have:

- at least 3 genuinely edit-worthy Killua shots
- at least 3 genuinely edit-worthy Eren shots
- at least 2 shots containing BOTH characters
- one excellent transformation
- one excellent Killua speed reveal
- one excellent final poster composition

If those do not exist:

the action design is not finished.

==================================================
30. MOST IMPORTANT RULE
==================================================

DO NOT ASK:

"How many soldiers can we put on screen?"

ASK:

"How hard does this shot LOOK?"

One perfectly composed:

Killua standing on tank
+
Titan Eren emerging through smoke

is worth more than:

100 random NPCs
+
20 explosions
+
unreadable chaos.

CLARITY + AURA + SCALE + IMPACT.

==================================================
FINAL CREATIVE TARGET
==================================================

The finished video should make viewers want to comment things like:

"Killua is lowkey scarier than the Titan 😭"

"Bro Eren in the background while Killua walks 💀"

"The tank scene was HARD"

"Imagine being a soldier and seeing this"

"Why did Killua have so much aura"

"Part 2"

This should feel like:

A CINEMATIC CROSSOVER
+
AN ANIME EDIT
+
A WAR SIMULATION

all at once.

PUSH THE ACTION DIRECTION HARD.

Do not settle for technically functional animation.

MAKE THE SHOTS COOL.
````
