PROJECT CONCEPT
==================================================

TITLE:

WHAT IF FRICTION
DISAPPEARED
FOR 60 SECONDS?

The viewer experiences a normal city where solid-surface friction suddenly drops
to effectively zero for exactly 60 seconds.

People lose traction.

Moving vehicles cannot brake or steer properly.

Stopped vehicles cannot effectively accelerate through tire grip.

Loose objects begin sliding wherever gravity or another force gives them a reason to move.

Slopes become extremely dangerous.

Mechanical systems that depend on friction begin failing.

Then, after 60 seconds:

FRICTION SUDDENLY RETURNS.

Objects and vehicles that are already moving suddenly regain grip.

This creates the final catastrophic payoff.

==================================================
IMPORTANT PHYSICS SCOPE
==================================================

For scientific coherence, define the scenario as:

SOLID-SURFACE FRICTION BETWEEN OBJECTS ≈ 0

Do NOT remove every form of molecular/internal friction in the universe.

Keep:

- air resistance
- fluid viscosity
- normal structural integrity
- normal gravity
- normal electricity
- normal human biology

This prevents the scenario from becoming physically undefined.

We are specifically exploring what happens when:

STATIC FRICTION ≈ 0
KINETIC FRICTION ≈ 0

between solid contacting surfaces.

==================================================
CORE PHYSICS RULES
==================================================

Important:

Objects do NOT randomly start moving merely because friction disappears.

Something must create a force.

Examples:

- person tries to walk
- car is already moving
- object is on a slope
- wheel is spinning
- cargo has inertia
- someone pushes something
- gravity pulls downhill

If an object is sitting perfectly still on a perfectly level surface and no
horizontal force acts on it, it should stay still.

Do not violate this simply for spectacle.

PHYSICS IMPLEMENTATION
==================================================

Build a deterministic simplified physics model.

We do not need a full scientific rigid-body engine if that becomes unstable.

Use controlled physics/animation where needed.

For each relevant object track:

position
velocity
mass approximation
surface normal
slope force
friction state

When friction = 0:

do not apply tangential friction forces.

For slope:

F_parallel = m * g * sin(theta)

Objects should accelerate naturally downhill.

For moving cars:

maintain existing horizontal velocity approximately,
subject to air resistance if modeled.

Steering should have almost no effect without lateral tire friction.

For braking:

wheel rotation may slow,
but chassis velocity should not meaningfully decrease from tire-road contact.

When friction returns:

restore traction forces carefully.

Do not numerically explode the simulation.

Clamp unrealistic impulses when necessary for cinematic stability.
