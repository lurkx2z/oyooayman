# The Boy Who Returned: plan

45 s, 1920x1080, 24 fps. Prehistoric village at dawn. Aren (about 14) comes home after he was taken for the
sacrifice; his father (about 44) recognises him and steps back. Three villagers notice at the end.

The heart of the scene, and the part to judge first: **Aren smiles, his father recognises him, his father steps
backward, Aren's smile fades** (12-26.5 s).

## Blocking (world metres, +Y is north)

- Father's fire `village.FIRE` (1.45, -0.6); father starts at F0 (2.10, 0.05), facing the embers, a stick in his right hand.
- Aren walks in from the north-west, (-5.6, 6.2) to A1 (-0.95, 0.30), arriving at 10.4 s, exhausted and limping
  (one stumble). Two hesitant steps to about (-0.3, 0.3) at 16.0-17.2 s; one more step to A3 (0.36, 0.16) at 19.0-20.0 s.
- The father's step back: right (back) foot lifts 20.80, lands 21.35 about 38 cm behind; the weight moves onto it
  21.2-21.8; the shoulders follow late 21.35-22.0; the front foot drags after it 22.05-22.5. About 2 m between them after.
- Villagers north of the fire, busy with their backs half turned: Neda (1.3, 5.4) twisting cord, the elder
  (0.55, 5.95), the hunter at the spear rack (-0.9, 4.9). They react at 38.7-39.0 s; Neda whispers to the elder,
  the hunter steps back towards the spears.
- Line of action: every camera stays south of the Aren-father line, so Aren is always screen-left, his father
  screen-right. The low sun is in the east-north-east: it lights Aren's face, it rims the father, whose face is lit
  warm from the fire.

## Sound

Dawn wind and leaves, sparse distant birds, the crackle of the fire (louder when the camera is near it), every
footstep from the animation's foot plants (dragging scuffs while Aren is exhausted, a heavier landing for the step
back), cloth moves, breath that follows each character's animated breathing (held while the father steps back),
the stick hitting the stones and a burst of sparks, villagers' whispers. Score: a low D drone, a hopeful chord and a
voice-like line over Aren's smile that rises towards the embrace and stops dead when the father's foot lifts;
sparse low piano after; strings return under "It's me" and swell slowly into the pull back. Mix at -15 LUFS
integrated, true peak under -1 dBTP.
